About My Physics Academia

Understand Physics. Explore the Universe.

Our Mission

To make Class 11 and Class 12 Physics clearer and more approachable through structured, concept-based learning.

Our Vision

A learning environment where students build genuine understanding of physical ideas and develop curiosity about the universe.

Why a concept-first approach?

Physics is not only a collection of formulas. It is a way to describe and understand how the natural world behaves. A formula becomes more meaningful when a learner understands the physical idea behind it, the quantities involved and the conditions in which it applies.

My Physics Academia is built around that principle: begin with the concept, connect it to mathematical reasoning, and then explore its use in derivations and numerical problems.

How Physics learning is organised

The intended learning path connects four parts of studying a topic:

1. Understand the idea

Start by identifying the physical concept and what it explains.

2. Build the model

Connect the concept with diagrams, quantities, units and mathematical relationships.

3. Follow the reasoning

Work through the logic behind important equations and derivations instead of memorising results alone.

4. Apply the concept

Use the idea in worked examples and numerical problem-solving.

Become the Protagonist

One Universe, Connected Stories
