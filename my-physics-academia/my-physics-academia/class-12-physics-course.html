About this Class 12 Physics course

Study electricity, magnetism, electromagnetic waves, optics and modern Physics through a structured chapter-wise course. Lessons are designed to develop conceptual understanding, support derivations and strengthen numerical problem-solving.

What you will learn

  • Understand electric charges, fields, potential and capacitance.
  • Analyse electric circuits and current electricity.
  • Explain magnetic fields, forces and electromagnetic induction.
  • Understand alternating current and electromagnetic waves.
  • Apply ray optics and wave optics principles.
  • Explore quantum ideas, atomic structure, nuclei and semiconductors.
