About this Class 11 Physics course

Build a strong foundation in Physics through mechanics, properties of matter, heat, oscillations and waves. The course is organised chapter by chapter, with clear explanations, derivations and solved numerical problems.

What you will learn

  • Understand physical quantities, units and measurement.
  • Analyse motion using vectors and equations of motion.
  • Apply Newton’s laws, work-energy principles and momentum.
  • Understand rotational motion and gravitation.
  • Explain properties of solids, fluids and thermal systems.
  • Study oscillations and wave motion using physical models.
