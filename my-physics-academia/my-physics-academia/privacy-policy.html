Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how My Physics Academia (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you visit www.myphysicsacademia.in, create a student account, access course content or contact support.

2. Information we handle

  • Account information: name and email address provided when a student account is created.
  • Authentication information: login and session information handled through Supabase Authentication. Passwords are submitted to the authentication provider; we do not display or intentionally store plain-text passwords.
  • Course and access information: account identifier, course access status and learning content access needed to provide enrolled courses.
  • Support communications: information you choose to send by email or WhatsApp, such as your contact details and the contents of your message.
  • Technical and usage information: browser/device details, pages visited and interaction events may be collected by analytics tags configured through Google Tag Manager. The information collected depends on the tags enabled in that container.

3. Why we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, authenticate students, provide course access, protect the platform, respond to support requests, understand site performance and improve the learning experience. We do not ask students to send passwords or payment-card details through support messages.

4. Services that help operate the website

  • Supabase: authentication and database services used for student accounts and course-access records.
  • Mux: video hosting and playback services for course videos.
  • Google Tag Manager and tags configured within it: website measurement and interaction analytics, depending on the active container configuration.
  • Email and WhatsApp: support channels when you choose to contact us through those services.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Their systems may process data in locations outside your state or country, subject to their service arrangements and applicable law.

5. Payments

As of the date above, online checkout/payment processing is not enabled on this website. We do not currently collect card or bank payment details through the website. If a payment provider is introduced, this policy and the checkout information will be updated to identify the provider and explain the relevant processing before payment is accepted.

6. Children and student accounts

Our learning content is intended for Class 11 and Class 12 students, some of whom may be under 18. If you are under 18, ask a parent or legal guardian to review this policy and assist you with creating and using an account. Parents or guardians who have questions about a student account or its information can contact us using the details below. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive personal information.

7. Data security and retention

We use service-provider security features and access controls intended to protect account and course information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain account/course records, respond to requests, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. Specific retention periods may depend on the type of record and provider.

8. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information associated with your account, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify the request and may retain limited records where required for security, legal or legitimate operational reasons. Account deletion requests can be sent to the support email below.

9. External links

The website may link to third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites or services that we do not operate. Review their privacy notices before using them.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when website features, service providers or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@myphysicsacademia.in or WhatsApp +91 90436 37266.
